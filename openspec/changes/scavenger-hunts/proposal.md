# Proposal: Scavenger Hunts

## Problem
Visitors book a site, dig, and leave. There's no in-visit structure or reason to return to the same site. Operators have no tool to differentiate their site beyond minerals and price.

## Proposed Solution
Operators define a scavenger hunt for their site — a list of minerals or geological features to find. Visitors work through the list during or after their visit and mark items as found. Completing a hunt earns a bonus passport stamp and points toward badges.

## Why This Works for Digby
- Uses existing primitives: sites, bookings, passport stamps, minerals
- Gives operators a creative tool to make their site stickier
- Gives visitors a reason to book the same site multiple times (new hunt variations)
- Naturally extends into seasonal hunts, themed hunts, difficulty tiers

## User Stories
- As an **operator**, I can create a scavenger hunt for my site with a list of items to find
- As a **visitor**, I can view the active hunt for a site I've booked
- As a **visitor**, I can check off items I've found during or after my visit
- As a **visitor**, I earn a bonus passport stamp when I complete a hunt
