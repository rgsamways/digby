# Proposal: Points Economy (Round 1)

## What
Extend the passport/badge system into a full points economy. Visitors earn points from activity; points are displayed on their passport and a public leaderboard shows the top rockhounds.

## Why
The passport page currently shows stamps and badges but gives no reason to return. Points create a persistent score that grows with engagement and makes the leaderboard a social hook — visitors can see where they rank among the community.

## Scope (Round 1 only)
- Earn points: stamps, unique mineral discoveries, completed scavenger hunts
- Display total points prominently on the passport page
- Public leaderboard (top 10) on the passport page
- No perks, discounts, or redemptions yet (Round 2+)
