# Proposal: Mineral Identification Quiz

## What
A 10-question multiple-choice quiz drawn from a 25-question bank about Ontario minerals, rockhounding, and geology. Points feed into the passport economy. No images required — text-only questions.

## Why
Completely standalone (no booking dependency), fun differentiator, and drives repeat visits. The question bank can be written from domain knowledge — no content pipeline needed. Points integration makes the leaderboard feel alive without real bookings.

## Scoring
- 5 pts per correct answer
- +25 pts bonus for perfect score (10/10)
- Max per session: 75 pts
- Cumulative quiz points included in passport total

## UX Flow
Intro screen → 10 questions one at a time → show answer + explanation after each → results screen with pts earned → link to passport
