Print Specification — Hannah Lithographers

Size
3.5 × 2 inches (standard business card)
Bleed
Add 0.125" bleed on all sides — extend background colours to bleed edge
Corners
Rounded corners — 3/16" radius die cut
Stock
16pt or 18pt card stock. Coated matte for a refined feel; uncoated natural/cream for an earthy feel. Either suits the brand well.
Finish
Matte laminate on both sides. Optional: spot UV on the "digby.rocks" wordmark on the front for a subtle premium touch.
Colours
Full colour both sides (4/4). Front is predominantly dark — ensure press profile handles deep blacks well.
Quantity
250 to start. The back illustration is a conversation piece — people will keep these.
