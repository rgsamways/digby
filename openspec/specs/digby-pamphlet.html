New to rockhounding?
Your first dig starts here.
Ontario's geology is genuinely remarkable — and you don't need a geology degree to appreciate it. digby makes your first dig safe, productive, and deeply satisfying. We'll tell you exactly where to go, what to look for, and what you found.
  • Curated beginner-friendly sites with step-by-step guidance
  • AI mineral identifier — photograph it, know what it is instantly
  • Starter gear kits — hammer, chisel, safety glasses, field guide
  • Junior Geologist programme for kids — games, badges, and real finds
  • Plain-language geology education tied to every site
For families
Kids who dig for rocks remember it for life. Our Junior Geologist programme turns every site visit into a learning adventure — and builds real geological literacy along the way.
Experienced collector or professional?
Built to your standard.
digby is built on OGS data, designed for real fieldwork, and takes geological accuracy seriously. It's a platform for people who know what a P.Geo is — and for those who want to become one someday. No dumbing down.
  • Interactive geology map with full OGS bedrock and MDI layers
  • GIS data export — GeoJSON, shapefile, CSV, GeoPackage
  • Expert verification system for citizen science data
  • Formation-level analytics on the community find dataset
  • Legacy data digitisation — your field notebooks, finally searchable
  • API access for integration with your own GIS workflow
Citizen science
Every find logged on digby contributes to a growing georeferenced mineral dataset for Ontario — GPS-located, formation-tagged, and built toward a future data-sharing partnership with the OGS.
Built in Ontario, for Ontario.
digby was founded by a University of Windsor geography and geology graduate who took a four-day field identification trip through the Grenville Province and never forgot it. This platform is the direct descendant of that experience — built to give it to anyone who wants it.
