What is digby?
A platform built for every rockhound in Ontario.
digby.rocks brings together site booking, an AI mineral identifier, and an interactive geology map built on OGS data — so you can find great sites, understand what you find, and connect with Ontario's geology community. Whether you're on your first dig or your five-hundredth.
🪨
Book a Dig Site
Curated sites across Ontario with geological context — know what you're looking for and why it's there before you arrive.
🔍
Identify Your Finds
Photograph a specimen and get a detailed identification — mineral properties, Ontario localities, UV behaviour, and what else to look for nearby.
🗺️
Explore Ontario Geology
An interactive map layering OGS bedrock geology, mineral occurrences, and digby sites — so you understand the ground you're standing on.
🛒
Gear Shop
Hammers, chisels, UV lamps, loupes, field guides, and specimen boxes — everything you need, curated for Ontario rockhounding.
Who it's for
Beginners
New to rockhounding? Guided sites, AI identification, and learning resources get you started right.
Collectors
Experienced collectors get GIS-grade geology data, export tools, and a find journal that grows with your collection.
Families
Junior Geologist games and guided sites make this a genuine outdoor learning experience for kids.
Professionals
P.Geo and GIS professionals: data export, API access, and a citizen science dataset worth contributing to.
