## Part 2: Digby.rocks — The Rockhound Booking App

### The Core Idea

An **Airbnb-style marketplace** for rockhound site access in Ontario (and eventually Canada-wide). Connects landowners and guided tour operators with rockhound visitors who want to book legitimate, paid access to mineral-rich private properties and guided experiences.

### Why Bancroft?

- Nearly 90% of all mineral types found on Earth are present in North Hastings
- Over 1,600 identified mineral species in the Bancroft area
- Tens of thousands of rockhound enthusiasts travel here annually
- The Bancroft Rockhound Gemboree draws 100+ dealers from around the globe each July
- Operators like Dark Star Crystal Mines, Princess Sodalite Mine already do pay-to-dig — but manage bookings manually through their own small websites

### The Problem Being Solved

- Rockhounds must contact individual landowners/operators separately — no central booking platform
- Landowners have no easy way to monetize access to their mineral-rich properties
- Ontario law requires permission to collect on private land — but getting that permission is friction-heavy
- Visiting rockhounds (many from the US) have no single discovery + booking resource for Canada

### Competitive Landscape

|App/Platform            |What It Does                           |Gap                          |
|------------------------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
|Ruby Glint              |Photo rock ID + 260K location map      |No booking, no Canada focus  |
|Rockhounding.org        |1,000+ GPS-verified US sites, community|US only, no transactions     |
|Rockhounding Spots (iOS)|260K US localities, filtering          |US only, no booking          |
|Rockd                   |Geological maps + social check-ins     |Geology tool, not booking    |
|OnX Hunt                |Property ownership lookup for hunters  |Not purpose-built, no booking|
|Dark Star Crystal Mines |Pay-to-dig on their own claims         |Single operator, siloed      |
|Destination Ontario     |Lists Ontario sites                    |Static, no booking           |

**The gap:** No platform combines Ontario/Canada focus + private land booking + payment + availability management. This is the lane.

### Revenue Model

- Commission per booking: 10–15% of booking fee
- Operators set their own prices (typically $12–$50/person for pay-to-dig)
- Future: premium operator listings, guided tour upsells, mineral ID features

### MVP Feature Set (To Be Designed)

- Operator/landowner portal: list site, set availability, set price, manage bookings
- Visitor-facing: map view of bookable sites, species info per site, calendar booking, payment
- Mobile-first (iOS + Android)
- Basic mineral species data tied to each site location

### Year One App Goals

- Build MVP by month 6–8
- 10–20 operators/landowners listed by end of year
- Prove booking model works
- Revenue target year one: $2,000–$5,000 (validation stage)
- Year 2–3: expand to all Ontario, then Canada

## Part 3: Grants & Funding

### Most Relevant (Apply Early)

**Starter Company Plus**

- Up to $5,000 grant + mentorship coaching
- Eligibility: Ontario resident, 18+, contribute 25% match
- Best use: App development seed costs (hosting, design, domain tools)
- Apply through local Small Business Centre

**Digital Main Street (DMS) Grant**

- $2,500 for digital transformation
- If operating a local IT services shop

**Canada-Ontario Jobs Grant (COJG)**

- Up to $10,000/trainee for third-party training
- Useful if hiring and training someone

**DMAP (Digital Modernization and Adoption Plan)**

- For SMEs 1–499 employees
- Can be framed around the Digby.rocks tech platform as a tourism digital product

**CanExport SMEs**

- Up to $50,000 (50% of costs) for exporting services internationally
- Relevant once app has US/international users

### Important Caution on EODF

The Eastern Ontario Development Fund explicitly **excludes** consulting businesses and information/communication services from eligibility. However, a software *product* company (Digby.rocks as a marketplace) may qualify. Confirm with a regional advisor.

### Local Support

- Bancroft Community Improvement Plan: loans, grants, waived fees, tax assistance
- Free Enterprise Facilitator business coaching through Bancroft Chamber of Commerce
- Community Futures Development Corporation (Bancroft): additional grants and loans
